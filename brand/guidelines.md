# Rituel brand guidelines

Distilled from the official brand boards ([`images/brand-book-1.png`](images/brand-book-1.png), [`images/brand-book-2.png`](images/brand-book-2.png)). For generated-image rules see [`image-style.md`](image-style.md).

## Story & voice

Rituel is a modern fintech brand that makes money feel simple, human, and empowering — smart technology plus a deep understanding of people's financial lives. Taglines: **"Modern Money for a Brighter Tomorrow"** / **"Smarter Finance. A Brighter Future."** Tone: clean, modern, approachable; confident without jargon.

## Logo

Wordmark: bold rounded sans "Rituel" with a four-point star inside the R counter and a sparkle as the i-dot. Masters live in `public/logo.svg` (ink) and `public/logo-dark.svg` (white); raster copies in [`logo/`](logo/).

- Clearspace: at least one X (cap-height width of the mark) on all sides.
- Variants: full color (on Rituel Purple tile), monochrome light (white on dark), monochrome dark (black on light), app icon (rounded-square R + sparkle).
- Never: recolor, stretch, add effects, or place on busy/low-contrast backgrounds.
- Generated illustrations never contain the logo or any text — brand assets are composited at the layout level, not baked into AI imagery.

## Typography

**Graphik** for brand and marketing materials: Light, Regular, Medium, Semibold, Bold. Traits: clean, modern, approachable — "clarity and confidence to everything we say." The product website renders Geist Sans/Mono; treat that as the app implementation, not a brand conflict.

## Iconography & graphic elements

Rounded line icons (analytics, security, payments, cards) on soft tiles. Recurring graphics: the four-point sparkle, frosted-glass 3D cards and payment objects, thin outlined rings, glow orbs, diagonal gradient meshes. Composition favors generous empty space and a single clear subject.
